## 📚 From Programming Fundamentals to DBMS

Since the start of my second semester, I was introduced to a new subject called **Database**, formally known as **DBMS (Database Management System)**. In my first semester, my core subject was Programming Fundamentals, and now the focus has shifted. Interestingly, the same instructor who taught us Programming Fundamentals is now teaching us DBMS as well.

At the beginning, I had absolutely no idea what this subject was about. I didn’t have any prior knowledge or even interest in it 😅. Since I wasn’t a top scorer in Programming Fundamentals and often struggled to understand certain concepts, I felt even more anxious about DBMS. I constantly worried whether I would be able to understand or solve anything. However, after doing some basic research, I slowly started gaining a little understanding.

## 🏫 Classes, Concepts & Practical Learning

When the semester officially started, our journey began. Initially, we had a sports gala followed by Ramadan, but our physical classes continued. During this time, our instructor covered essential topics such as:

* **Primary Keys**
* **Foreign Keys**
* **File Systems**

These concepts turned out to be quite interesting and very useful in real-life applications 💡.

### The Lab Experience
Alongside theory, we had lab sessions where **Sir Bilal** guided us. He introduced us to a project early on: creating a website to sell products. While my group selected a topic, the direction shifted toward e-commerce and machine learning model training, and for now, that project is on pause.

Since we haven’t been formally taught website development or blogging yet, I started building my own website independently—and this blog post is a part of that journey 🌐.

## 🌙 Challenges, Online Shift & Growing Confidence

In the beginning, DBMS felt difficult and confusing, but my interest grew thanks to our instructor’s teaching style. He explains concepts in such a simple way that understanding becomes much easier. While I'm still learning, DBMS definitely feels more approachable now 😊.

However, just as we were getting into the flow, classes shifted from physical to **online** due to unfavorable conditions. Online learning is naturally more difficult due to the lack of interaction, but our instructor supported us through proper lectures, labs, and evaluations.

### Navigating Ramadan and Exams
Maintaining a rhythm during Ramadan and online setup was challenging. Some students faced technical issues during evaluations—like microphone problems—which led to some cancellations. Honestly, that worked slightly in my favor! 😅

Now, online classes are ending, and **midterm exams** have started. Although I feel a bit nervous about the theory and lab evaluations, I am prepared and determined to give my best 💪✨.
